# Status: FormatoKit 0.22.0 <!-- x-release-please-version -->

FormatoKit 0.21.0 (2026-11-25), against RosettaSciIO 0.14.0 (38 plugins on disk: 2 present, 7 partial, 29 missing, `docs/parity.md`). Live: Gatan DM3/DM4 (scan pixel size on the spectrum images, a scan-shaped rank-3 object read as 4D, the camera pixel pitch), TIFF
and tifffile's shaped series (0.11.0; the Zeiss SEM, TVIPS and JEOL SightX pixel sizes and the GMS pixel pitch since 0.12.0, a series the reader cannot place refused by name), MRC, Merlin MIB (pinned byte for byte, including the app convention, which also reads a header without or against its detector shape, and a multi-file save as one scan), EMPAD, MSA/EMSA, Velox EMD (images, spectrum images with live and real time, the optics and `Sample.elements`, EELS spectrum images with beam energy and angles, single spectra with their elements, metadata groups and segment times,
acquisition metadata with the pixel size), generic HDF5 read (with h5py's LZF filter), append and write sessions (attributes of text, integers, reals and h5py's boolean, `setBoolAttribute`; boolean datasets, scalar `writeBool` and one-dimensional `writeBools`, as orix's `is_in_data`), the BraggVectors writer, TIA .ser series and their .emi (`FormatoTIA`, 0.20.0: `TIAReader.read/header` pinned exact to RosettaSciIO on its 28 public .ser files, `TIAReader.readEMI` to HyperSpy on its 21 public .emi files: units, date and mapped metadata; `FormatoLoader` and `Peek` know them), HyperSpy files written and read (`FormatoHSPY`, 0.19.0: `HSPYWriter.write` for a signal in memory and `HSPYWriter.open` for a cube written a slab at a time over FormatoHDF5's `HDF5SlabWriter`, layout 3.3 pinned to what HyperSpy 2.5.0 writes, no compression; `HSPYReader.experiments/read/readStored` for rank 1 to 4, one or several experiments, gzip or not, pinned exact to RosettaSciIO 0.14.0's reading, ragged, complex and non-uniform signals refused by name), `FormatoLoader.sniff/load` over them (`FormatoFileKind.hyperSpy` for `.hspy`), EBSD patterns and maps (`FormatoEBSD`, FormatoKit#147: h5ebsd in its four layouts, Oxford `.ebsp`, EDAX `.up1`/`.up2` and NORDIF patterns read by record as a lazy signal with their header; `.ang`, `.ctf`, map-only H5OINA, orix HDF5 and the Bruker, EDAX and EMsoft map files as plain columns with their phase headers, pinned exact to kikuchipy 0.13.1 and orix 0.15.0; the `.ang` and `.ctf` writers), and `Peek` (one decimated frame and the header facts of a file, for Quick Look). The app image readers (`TIFFAppImage`, `MRCAppImage`, `DM4Experiment.readImage`) also read at the stored bit depth (`readStored`, `readStoredImage`: a `FormatoArray` of the file's element type, no Float32 copy on the way; `FormatoArray.floats()` widens it for a computation). The 4D datacube readers hand out tiles at the stored bit depth too: `HDF5Datacube` and EMPAD (float32) always, and the app conventions of `DM4Datacube` and `MIBReader` with `keepStoredType: true`. Timings against
RosettaSciIO: `docs/benchmarks.md`; what is open: the GitHub issues (the GMS EDS geometry needs a real file; DM and TIFF read speed); known limits: `docs/parity.md`.

Since 0.11.0: 0.12.0 (2026-10-31) added the readers above, bounded the Velox metadata and frame counts a file can ask for, dropped the internal-only public helpers and tiled the DM4 scan-fastest gather (whole scan 420 ms to 68 ms); 0.12.1 (2026-11-01) made the reader notes state what is known; 0.13.0 (2026-11-11) reads Merlin's multi-file save as one scan, its shape from Frames per Trigger; 0.14.0 (2026-11-11) reads HDF5 datasets compressed with h5py's LZF filter (AZtec's `.h5oina` patterns), through a decode-only port of liblzf (`NOTICE`); 0.19.0 (2026-11-23) writes and reads HyperSpy files; 0.20.0 (2026-11-24) reads TIA .ser files and their .emi, and `FormatoFileKind` gains `.tia` (breaking: an exhaustive switch needs a `.tia` arm); 0.20.1 (2026-11-24) refuses a .ser whose shape holds more elements than its series and finds an .emi's .ser files by name; 0.21.0 (2026-11-25) writes an array of booleans as h5py stores a NumPy bool array. The helpers deprecated for removal at 0.13.0 (`FormatoAxis.isBitwiseEqual`, `AcquisitionMetadata.pythonRound` and `numpyDegrees`, `FormatoArray.checkSlab` and `forEachRun`) are gone. Details and the migration notes: `CHANGELOG.md`.

Test split (2026-10-21): the comparisons with the pinned RosettaSciIO and Pint values (`PinsTests`, `SpectrumImagePinsTests`, `UnitsTests`, `FormatoTIFFPinsTests`) and the inherited golden baseline are the `FormatoKitParityTests` target (fixtures stay in `Tests/Formato*Tests/Fixtures`, found through `FormatoKitTestSupport`); `tools/test.sh --parallel --skip FormatoKitParityTests` (or `swift test -c release --parallel --skip FormatoKitParityTests`) is the per-session gate (380 tests), plain `tools/test.sh --parallel` the full suite (396 tests: 380 fast + 16 parity, counted with `swift test list` at 0.11.0). The HDF5 exit-barrier test needs `tools/test.sh` (it sets `FORMATO_HDF5_PATH`). Timings of 2026-10-21 (Mac, release, warm build): fast target 8.2 s, full suite 9.4 s through `tools/test.sh`.
